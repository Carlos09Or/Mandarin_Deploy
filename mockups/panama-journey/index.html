A journey across Panama

Learning Mandarin while travelling Panama by train and discovering its history through China. Each of the 10 units is a province; finishing one earns a passport stamp and the train carries you to the next. Click any phone to view it full size (scroll inside route screens). Sample content is illustrative and historical text is a draft that needs review.

1 · Map overview (home)

Option B layout: a large, swipeable map centered on where you are, plus a mini-map of the whole country. Completed provinces are painted and stamped, the current one glows with the train, locked ones sit under fog. Indigenous comarcas appear with their names as neutral sand-colored regions.

2 · Train travel

After the province checkpoint: the stamp lands, the train rolls along the route to the next province and the fog lifts. About 3 seconds, skippable. Frame 2 animates in the full-size view.

3 · Province route

Inside a province the dots become a railway through local scenery: lessons are station stops with real place names, a reading appears as a book stop, and the unit review is the province's landmark.

4 · Passport

The passport replaces gems as the big reward: one page per province, a stamp when you finish it, and a postcard with a cultural fact and a bonus phrase in Chinese that you can share.

5 · Reading (Lectura)

Short illustrated stories about Panama's history seen through China. English narrates; short HSK 1 sentences carry the Chinese, with taps for new words and quick interactions. Sample: "The railroad and the first Chinese in Panama".

6 · Living map

The same home map follows the real clock and calendar: day, sunset, night with city lights, light rain in the rainy season (May–November), and flags along the line in November, the patriotic month.
