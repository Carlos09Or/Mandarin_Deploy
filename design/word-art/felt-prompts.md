# Ilustraciones de fieltro · prompts por tanda

Adjunta SIEMPRE `style-reference.webp` (el niño, la niña y el cerdito) y pega primero el bloque de estilo
seguido de una tanda de 3. Una tanda por mensaje.

## Bloque de estilo (va al inicio de cada mensaje)

```
Use your IMAGE GENERATION tool (do not draw with code, SVG or shapes). Create ONE image with THREE separate square illustrations side by side, in exactly the style of the attached reference image: handmade layered felt and paper cut-outs, soft drop shadows between layers, subtle fabric texture, rounded edges, warm friendly characters with round faces, dot eyes and rosy cheeks, Panamanian details (sombrero pintao, pollera with red embroidery, montuno shirt, red hibiscus, tropical leaves). Same level of detail and richness as the reference, never simple icons. Palette: navy #0a426d, ocean blue #2f88d3, turquoise #34b1da, jungle green #206241, leaf green #60a554, gold #f2c14e, Panama red #d21034, sand #f2dbbd, cream #f5e3ac. Each illustration on a plain white background, the subject placed where the matching part sits in the Chinese character (left / right / top / bottom). No text, no letters, no Chinese characters in the image.
```
